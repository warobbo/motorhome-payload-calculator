Cookies

Last updated 15 October 2026. This notice is published by Motorhome Payload Calculator.

This site does not use tracking cookies. It does not set analytics cookies or advertising cookies, and it does not set a cookie of its own to remember you.

Local storage is not a cookie

Calculator figures and tyre figures are stored in this browser’s local storage. They stay on this device and are not filled in until you restore them. A key typed into the hidden setup form is stored the same way. A lookup can send that key with the registration. It is not written into a cookie. Clear site data in the browser to remove the saved copy.

Plate lookups

A UK registration lookup is a request, not a cookie. The page sends the registration to a Cloudflare Worker, and that Worker asks DVLA. The response is shown in the calculator. Nothing about that lookup is written into a cookie.

Typefaces

Outfit and Fraunces are font files on this site. The pages do not load Google Fonts. This site does not run an advertising or analytics tag.
