Terms

Last updated 15 October 2026. These terms cover the payload calculator and the tyre tool on Motorhome Payload Calculator.

An estimate, not a plated weight

The figures are a planning estimate. They are not a legal weighbridge ticket and they do not change the plated Maximum Authorised Mass (MAM). Always verify with a calibrated weighbridge. The VIN plate on your vehicle takes priority over anything this site shows.

Under MAM but over on an axle is still a fail. This site will not invent an axle split from people or kit.

Plate lookups

A UK registration lookup is sent to a Cloudflare Worker, which asks DVLA for make, year and revenue weight. That revenue weight is not a promise of Mass in Service, and it is not a substitute for the VIN plate. Demo plates are sample records, not live DVLA results.

Tyre pressures

The tyre tool shows a cold pressure only when a listed manufacturer table has one. If your size is not covered, it refuses a number. Tyre capacity is not permission to exceed the vehicle axle, wheel or legal limits. The tyre maker and a fitter take priority. Wrong pressure or an overloaded tyre can be dangerous.
