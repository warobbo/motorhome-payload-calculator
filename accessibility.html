Accessibility

Last updated 15 October 2026. This statement is published by Motorhome Payload Calculator for motorhomepayload.co.uk.

We want the payload calculator, the tyre tool, and these notices to be usable with a keyboard, a screen reader, and on a small screen.

What we aim for

The pages aim at the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.2 level AA. This statement is not a claim that an external audit has signed the site off. It describes how the pages are built and how to tell us when something blocks you.

How the pages are built

  • A skip link on each page moves to the main content.
  • The payload and tyre tools use labels tied to their fields, and the buttons have visible names.
  • Text and controls are meant to keep contrast against the page colours.
  • The layout works on a narrow screen and a wide one.
  • Typefaces are files on this site, with system fonts as a fallback.

Known limits

The calculator is a long form. Some results update as you type, and a dense pass can be hard work in a screen reader. A few links leave this site, including Motorhome Tools, the guides, a weighbridge map, and the contact form. Those sites have their own pages. We have not commissioned a formal accessibility audit.

Tell us

If a page blocks you, write through the contact form at motorhometools.co.uk/contact.html. Say the page address and what you were trying to do. We will use that to fix the page or to send the same information another way.

Enforcement

This site is not a public-sector body. If you are not happy with the response, you can contact the Equality Advisory and Support Service: equalityadvisoryservice.com.
